\begin{abstract}
    \cite{Long_Lag2025} advocate the use of long lags VARs for structural analysis, claiming that the truncation bias is a severe problem for the IRFs estimation.
    VARs can be used to create additional moments to estimate DSGE models minimizing the distance between their IRFs and therfore estimating the underlying structural parameters.
    In this study we ask what are the consequencies of enriching the lag structure of a VAR used for DSGE estimation.
    I replicate the results of \cite{ACEL2011} and I find that the both IRFs and Structural parameters are sensible to the chosen lag lenght.
    Matching the DSGE with a VAR(12) instead of the baseline VAR(4), I find that the Calvo Price parameters that differencieate the observationally equivalent \cite{ACEL2011} model between firm specific capital vs homogeneous capital are no longer statistically different from each other. 
    The implied duration of the Calvo Price parameter is 2.5 times higher than the baseline, re opening the micro-macro discrepancy in price stickiness.
    
    \vspace{0.3cm}
    \noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Truncation Bias, Long Lag VARs, DSGE Estimation, Minimum Distance, IRFs
    \\
    \noindent\textbf{JEL Codes:} C32, C52, E32
\end{abstract}
